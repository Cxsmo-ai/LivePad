## 1. Quick Start (How To Play)
**Just type what you want the character to do in chat!**
You can type one word, or string multiple words together in ONE message for combos. No passwords or direct connection needed.

### Popular Quick Combos:
- `w` — Walk forward
- `w sprint` — Sprint forward full speed!
- `ads fire` — Aim down sights and shoot weapon
- `w sprint ads fire right 40` — Run, aim, shoot, and turn right all at once!

## 3. Numbers & Modifiers (Speed & Time)
No percentage (%) symbol needed! Just write the number. You can also specify seconds (`s`) or milliseconds (`ms`).

- `right 50` — Turns camera right at **50%** speed.
- `w 80` — Walks forward at **80%** stick tilt.
- `w 1.5s` — Walks forward for **1.5 seconds**.
- `w 80 1.2s` — Walks forward at **80%** strength for **1.2 seconds**!
- `fire 500ms` — Holds the shoot trigger for half a second.
- `buy 2s` — Holds interact button for 2 seconds (good for stubborn doors or Pack-a-Punch).

## 4. Multi-Action Combos
LivePad executes all actions in your message **simultaneously**!

- `w sprint` — Run forward full speed
- `w sprint slide` — Slide cancel / sliding sprint
- `ads fire` — Aim down sights and shoot
- `w ads fire` — Walk forward while aiming and shooting
- `w jump reload` — Jump forward and reload at the same time
- `w sprint ads fire right 40` — Sprint, aim, fire, and turn camera right

## 5. Playing with a Real Controller (LivePad Extension)
Viewers who want to use a real Xbox or PlayStation controller:
1. Load the **DeepAscension LivePad** extension in Edge or Chrome.
2. Open the stream chat.
3. Click **ARM CONTROLLER CHAT**.
4. Moving your physical controller automatically sends chat commands!
