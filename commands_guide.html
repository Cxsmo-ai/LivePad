1. The Quick Start (How To Play)

Just type what you want the character to do in chat!
You can type one word, or put a bunch of words together in ONE message to do crazy combos. No passwords or direct connection required.

w

Walk forward

w sprint

Sprint forward full speed

ads fire

Aim down sights and blast weapon

w sprint ads fire right 40

Run, aim, shoot, and turn right all at once!

Say it your way

walk, move, forward, w, fwd, and fw all move forward. back, reverse, retreat, s, bk, and bw all move backward. For combat, fire, shoot, attack, atk, f, and rt fire; ads, aim, zoom, aimdown, and lt aim.

One-word quick macros

Macros expand into simultaneous controller leases. Modifiers after a macro apply to its first action.

  • go / run / dash / rush → w sprint
  • runfire / runshoot / panic → w sprint fire
  • runaim → w sprint ads
  • runaimfire → w sprint ads fire
  • combat / fight / aimfire / shootaim → ads fire
  • jumpfire → jump fire
  • slidefire → w sprint slide fire
  • dodgeleft / dodgeright → strafe plus sprint

run 80 1.2s means w 80 1.2s sprint. You can also use plus signs: w+sprint+ads+fire. A leading ! is optional.

Short forms include fwd, bk, sl, cr, j, hop, rl, rel, sw, wpn, sp, m, g, gren, frag, t, i, pick, rev, lt, rt, f, lb, rb, l3, r3, du, dd, dl, and dr.

3. Speed & Time Modifiers (No % needed!)

You do not need any percentage symbol! Just write the number.
You can also add seconds like 1.2s or milliseconds like 500ms.

  • right 50 → Turns camera right at 50% speed.
  • w 80 → Walks forward at 80% stick tilt.
  • w 1.5s → Walks forward for 1.5 seconds.
  • w 80 1.2s → Walks forward at 80% strength for 1.2 seconds!
  • fire 500ms → Holds the shoot trigger for half a second.
  • buy 2s → Holds interact button for 2 seconds (good for stubborn doors or Pack-a-Punch).

4. Compound Combos (Type Multiple in One Message)

LivePad executes all actions in your message simultaneously! You don''t have to wait between commands.

w sprint

Run forward full speed

w sprint slide

Slide cancel / sliding sprint

ads fire

Aim down sights and shoot

w ads fire

Walk forward while aiming and shooting

w jump reload

Jump forward and reload at the same time

w sprint ads fire right 40

The ultimate COD combo: sprint, aim, fire, and turn camera right

5. Optional: Play with Real Controller (LivePad Extension)

If you don''t want to type and have a physical Xbox or PlayStation controller plugged into your PC:

  • Load the LivePad extension in Edge or Chrome.
  • Open the TikTok, YouTube, or Twitch live stream.
  • Click ARM CONTROLLER CHAT.
  • Now whenever you move your physical controller, LivePad converts your inputs into chat frames automatically!
