======================================================================
  HID Maestro - Streamer Edition (Quick Start Guide)
======================================================================

Control PC games (Call of Duty, Zombies, Rocket League, Elden Ring, or
any XInput / Xbox controller game) directly from TikTok LIVE and
YouTube LIVE chat comments, with zero latency and concurrent commands.

Everything needed to run this application is 100% self-contained in
this folder. You and your friends DO NOT need to install:
  - NO Python required
  - NO Node.js / npm required
  - NO .NET 10 or .NET SDK required (self-contained bridge bundled)
  - NO manual HIDMaestro driver or certificate installer required

----------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT TO SEND TO YOUR FRIEND
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Send the single zip file:
  HIDMaestroStreamerEdition-win-x64.zip

Tell your friend:
1. Extract the ZIP anywhere (e.g. Desktop).
2. Double-click "HIDMaestroStreamerEdition.exe".
3. When Windows asks for Administrator (UAC prompt), click YES.
   * On first launch, the app automatically deploys the self-signed
     HIDMaestro user-mode virtual device driver into Windows.
4. The window will open showing "Ready — Xbox 360". Done!

----------------------------------------------------------------------
SUPPORTED STREAM PLATFORMS (USE TOGETHER OR ON THEIR OWN)
----------------------------------------------------------------------
1. TikTok LIVE:
   - Check "[x] Enable TikTok Stream"
   - Type your TikTok username (e.g. @yourname or yourname)
   - Click "Connect TikTok"

2. YouTube LIVE (Powered by youtube-chat-next engine):
   - Check "[x] Enable YouTube Stream"
   - Enter your channel handle (e.g. @LofiGirl), video URL, or channel ID
   - Select Chat View: "Live Chat (all messages)" or "Top Chat"
   - Click "Connect YouTube"

3. Dual Mode (Together):
   - Check both boxes and click "CONNECT ALL ENABLED".
   - Comments from both platforms flow into one unified controller!
   - Every comment in the Unified Chat displays its platform logo:
     * TikTok cyan/magenta note logo for TikTok comments
     * YouTube red badge logo for YouTube comments

----------------------------------------------------------------------
COMMAND LIST & NUMBER MODIFIERS
----------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Movement (Left Stick):
   w / forward      - Move forward (Left stick UP)
   s / back         - Move backward (Left stick DOWN)
   a / left_strafe  - Strafe left (Left stick LEFT)
   d / right_strafe - Strafe right (Left stick RIGHT)
   sprint           - Sprint (Click L3 / Left Stick button)

2. Camera & Aiming (Right Stick with Analog Modifiers):
   left             - Look left (default 40% strength, 150ms)
   right            - Look right (default 40% strength, 150ms)
   up               - Look up (default 40% strength, 150ms)
   down             - Look down (default 40% strength, 150ms)

   Number Modifiers (Strength % 1-100):
     right 25       - Turn right gently (25% stick deflection)
     right 50       - Turn right medium (50% stick deflection)
     right 75       - Turn right fast (75% stick deflection)
     right 100      - Turn right maximum (100% stick deflection)
     left 40        - Turn left at 40% strength
     up 60          - Look up at 60% strength
     down 30        - Look down at 30% strength

   Duration Modifiers (Direction Strength% DurationMS):
     right 50 250   - Turn right at 50% strength for 250 milliseconds
     left 75 400    - Turn left at 75% strength for 400 milliseconds

3. Combat & Actions:
   fire / shoot     - Pull Right Trigger (RT, 100%)
   ads / aim        - Pull Left Trigger (LT, 100%)
   jump             - Button A (Green)
   crouch           - Button B (Red)
   reload           - Button X (Blue)
   swap             - Button Y (Yellow)
   melee            - Click R3 (Right Stick button)
   tac / tactical   - Left Bumper (LB)
   nade / grenade   - Right Bumper (RB)

----------------------------------------------------------------------
HOW TO DO COMBOS (CONCURRENT INPUT LEASES)
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Unlike older apps that sleep between buttons and take only one command,
HID Maestro uses concurrent input leases. Viewers can type compound
combos in a single comment:

  w sprint
  ads fire
  w fire
  w sprint ads fire right 35
  jump reload swap

When a viewer types:
  w sprint ads fire right 35

All 5 actions activate at the exact same instant:
  - Left Stick: 100% Forward
  - L3: Clicked (Sprint)
  - Left Trigger: 100% (Aim down sights)
  - Right Trigger: 100% (Fire weapon)
  - Right Stick: 35% Deflection Right (Smooth turning)

----------------------------------------------------------------------
SAFETY FEATURES & CONTROLS
----------------------------------------------------------------------
- Emergency Kill Switch (F12): Press F12 at any time in-game or in the
  app to instantly pause chat and neutralize all controller inputs.
- PAUSE CHAT: Temporarily ignores incoming stream comments.
- CLEAR CONTROLLER: Immediately centers sticks, releases buttons and
  triggers.
- Hardware Watchdog: If the stream freezes or Python stops responding,
  the C# bridge automatically resets the controller to neutral within
  1 second.
